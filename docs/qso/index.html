Waterfall

Audio frequency increases from left to right. The newest row is at the top, with a new row every 0.16 s. Brighter means a stronger signal. Horizontal lines mark the start of each 15 s slot, and decoded messages are labelled on their signals.

Scroll or pinch to zoom frequency, Alt+scroll to zoom time. Drag to move along the frequency axis or back to earlier slots. Double-click to show everything.

Band activity

dB: signal-to-noise ratio (approximate). DT: time offset of the signal in seconds, positive means late. Freq: audio frequency of the signal's lowest tone, in Hz.
